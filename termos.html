Privacy Policy

BENNER SISTEMAS values the privacy and protection of its employees' personal data. This policy describes how data is handled during the use of the SMS authentication system (2FA). The phone number is collected and used solely for sending one-time passcodes (OTP) via SMS for authentication and security purposes. Your phone number will never be used for marketing or promotional purposes.

  1. Data collected:
    • Full name
    • Phone number
    • Internal system access credentials
  2. Purpose of collection:

    The data collected is used exclusively for authentication of access to BENNER SISTEMAS's internal system, aiming to ensure the security of information and access.

  3. Data sharing:

    We do not share, sell, or provide the phone number or consent to receive messages to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Data may be shared only with technology partners for hosting and security purposes, or with public authorities when required by law.

  4. Opt-out:

    To stop receiving SMS messages, reply STOP to the received SMS.

  5. Charges notice:

    Messages and data may be charged by the employee's mobile carrier.

  6. Questions:

    If you have any questions, please contact BENNER SISTEMAS's IT department.
